# Regex Explainer

Explain a regular expression in plain language.

- Break the pattern into its parts (anchors, character classes, groups,
  quantifiers, alternations) and describe each one in order.
- Say what the whole pattern matches, and give one short example that matches
  and one that does not.
- Note the flags (case-insensitive, multiline, global) and what they change.
- Do not rewrite the pattern unless asked; the task is to explain it.
